# Edition 7 — *Celestial Concourse*

> **Version:** 7
> **Name:** Celestial Concourse
> **Epithet:** *The Zenith Era*
> **Mastery theme:** Delegate control
> **Status:** A complete snapshot carrying systems 1..7 of the mastery ladder.

## The mastery ladder

Airport Tycoon is one game played at eight depths. Edition **7** sits at depth
**7** of the ordered 1→8 ladder and introduces **Automation & Policy** (*Delegate control*). Each
edition keeps every earlier system and lights exactly one new one, so interest
multiplies as you climb. The whole series shares one **green-phosphor** look —
a monochrome 8-bit CRT palette with orthogonal, grid-aligned objects — so 1→8
read as a single machine; only the number of lit systems changes.

## New in Edition 7 — Automation & Policy

You stop pressing AUTO and start programming it. A configurable policy lets the tower run landing, de-icing, maintenance and booking on its own while you supervise the exceptions.

## What this edition adds

- A four-switch policy: auto-land, auto-de-ice, auto-maintain, auto-book
- The tower executes the policy every tick
- A delegated-count readout of how much is on autopilot
- Delegation of every earlier system, not just per-plane clicks

## Carried forward from Edition 6

Every earlier system remains part of Edition 7: Tower Operations, Service Premium & On-Time Combo, Weather & Runway Conditions, Fleet Health & Maintenance, Route Network & Reservations, Multi-Terminal Expansion. Edition 7 adds exactly one new system on top, so the player now holds **7** systems at once.
