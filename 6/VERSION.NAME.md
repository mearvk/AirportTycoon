# Edition 6 — *Grand Meridian*

> **Version:** 6
> **Name:** Grand Meridian
> **Epithet:** *The Continental Era*
> **Mastery theme:** Scale out
> **Status:** A complete snapshot carrying systems 1..6 of the mastery ladder.

## The mastery ladder

Airport Tycoon is one game played at eight depths. Edition **6** sits at depth
**6** of the ordered 1→8 ladder and introduces **Multi-Terminal Expansion** (*Scale out*). Each
edition keeps every earlier system and lights exactly one new one, so interest
multiplies as you climb. The whole series shares one **green-phosphor** look —
a monochrome 8-bit CRT palette with orthogonal, grid-aligned objects — so 1→8
read as a single machine; only the number of lit systems changes.

## New in Edition 6 — Multi-Terminal Expansion

The hub scales out across the map. You unlock continental terminals and balance load across them; the hub rewards keeping them level rather than overloading one.

## What this edition adds

- Up to four continental terminals you unlock in turn
- Departures routed to the least-loaded open terminal
- An imbalance readout that rewards level operations
- Each terminal behaves as its own small airport

## Carried forward from Edition 5

Every earlier system remains part of Edition 6: Tower Operations, Service Premium & On-Time Combo, Weather & Runway Conditions, Fleet Health & Maintenance, Route Network & Reservations. Edition 6 adds exactly one new system on top, so the player now holds **6** systems at once.
