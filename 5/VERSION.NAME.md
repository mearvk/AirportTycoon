# Edition 5 — *Imperial Horizons*

> **Version:** 5
> **Name:** Imperial Horizons
> **Epithet:** *The Grand Strategy Era*
> **Mastery theme:** Plan ahead
> **Status:** A complete snapshot carrying systems 1..5 of the mastery ladder.

## The mastery ladder

Airport Tycoon is one game played at eight depths. Edition **5** sits at depth
**5** of the ordered 1→8 ladder and introduces **Route Network & Reservations** (*Plan ahead*). Each
edition keeps every earlier system and lights exactly one new one, so interest
multiplies as you climb. The whole series shares one **green-phosphor** look —
a monochrome 8-bit CRT palette with orthogonal, grid-aligned objects — so 1→8
read as a single machine; only the number of lit systems changes.

## New in Edition 5 — Route Network & Reservations

Arrivals become a plan rather than a surprise. You book routes and reservations ahead; a full book smooths traffic and pays a steady bonus, an empty one starves the gates.

## What this edition adds

- A forward reservation book with a full-book target of 20
- A steady per-departure bonus while the book is well-stocked
- Departures draw the book down and count as fulfilled
- Booking you drive directly (or delegate in Edition 7)

## Carried forward from Edition 4

Every earlier system remains part of Edition 5: Tower Operations, Service Premium & On-Time Combo, Weather & Runway Conditions, Fleet Health & Maintenance. Edition 5 adds exactly one new system on top, so the player now holds **5** systems at once.
